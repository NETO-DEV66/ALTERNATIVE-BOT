## OpenHands Cloud conversation archiving
- `GET /api/v1/app-conversations?ids=<id>` → conversation record.
- `GET /api/v1/conversation/<id>/events/search?limit=100&page=N` → events, but
  **rate-limits (HTTP 429) around page 129** (~12,900 events).
- Better: `GET /api/v1/app-conversations/<id>/download` → a zip with `meta.json`
  + one `event_*.json` per event, in a single request. That is how 5765 events were saved.
